The problem

Babies born too early or too small can't hold their own body temperature. The usual fix is an incubator or a radiant warmer, and both need steady electricity. Many hospitals that care for newborns don't have it.

Cold is rarely the only cause when a newborn dies. It contributes to many of these deaths alongside infection, early birth and trouble breathing at birth (Lunze et al., 2013).

The Dream Warmer

Global Newborn Solutions makes the Dream Warmer, a reusable mattress filled with a material that melts at 37 °C, which is body temperature. It is heated with boiled water, then releases that heat slowly and holds a safe temperature for up to six hours. A built-in indicator shows when it is safe to place a baby on it. It needs no electricity.

It has been through six studies on three continents, including three clinical trials in Rwanda, and it is in use in more than a dozen countries.
